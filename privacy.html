Privacy Policy

VOLT Tickets (Discord bot) · Effective October 13, 2026

VOLT Tickets is a private Discord bot that manages support tickets for a single community. This policy explains what data the bot handles and why.

Data we handle

  • Discord identifiers: user IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs, used to link a ticket to its channel and owner and to check staff permissions.
  • Ticket records: the ID of the member who opened the ticket, ticket status (open, done, rated), the ticket category, the creation time, and whether the member left a rating.
  • Ticket statistics: simple counters such as the total and active number of tickets, used for internal reporting by the staff team.
  • Message content: the bot reads messages sent in the server to detect commands, configured auto-replies, and the rating channel. Message content is processed in memory and is not saved to our own storage.
  • Ticket transcripts: when a ticket is closed, the bot posts an HTML transcript of that ticket to a private staff channel inside Discord. The transcript stays on Discord and is not stored on our servers.

How we use data

We use this data only to run the ticket system: opening, managing, closing, and rating tickets, and keeping internal statistics. We do not sell data, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising.

AI and machine learning

We do not use message content or any other user data to train machine learning or AI models.

Storage and retention

Ticket records and statistics are stored in files on the bot's private server and are accessible only to the bot owner. We keep them for as long as they are needed to operate the ticket system, or until deletion is requested.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to delete the data linked to your Discord user ID, or to stop recording statistics for you. Contact us using the details below and we will act on the request within a reasonable time.

Children

The bot is intended for Discord users who meet Discord's minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
